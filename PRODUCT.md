# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS mockup (proposal for the redesign of the existing WordPress site bostonsoluxacademy.com). Integration into WordPress is a later step.

## Users

Decision-makers and professionals in French-speaking Africa (Togo, Tchad, Guinée Conakry, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinée Équatoriale) and beyond: DSI, RSSI, network/telecom engineers, project managers, bank and public-administration staff, plus HR/training managers who enrol teams. They come to find a certification course (ISO, ITIL, PMP, CCNA, ISA/IEC 62443…), check the next session date and city, and request enrolment or a quote.

## Product Purpose

BostonSolux Academy ("Excellence in Training") delivers certifying and non-certifying professional training, in person (Conakry, Lomé, Abidjan, N'Djaména, Malabo, Paris, Rockville) and via e-learning. The site must make finding a course fast and turn standards news (ISO, ITIL, ISA) into a reason to enrol.

## Positioning

Practitioner trainers, accredited certification bodies (PECB, PeopleCert, PMI, Cisco, EC-Council, ISACA), sessions held on the ground across West and Central Africa with offices in the USA and France.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prices are NEVER displayed. Calls to action are "Demander un devis", "S'inscrire", "Recevoir le programme".
- Header must contain a quick course search and a news section (actualités ISO / ITIL / ISA) next to it.
- News is entered manually by the team (blog posts).
- Existing menu: Accueil, À propos, Prochaines formations, Formations (certifiantes / non certifiantes), Nos événements, FAQ, Contacts; external E-learning and Blog.
- PMP enquiries: pmp@bostonsolux.com.

## Product Principles

1. Find a course in one gesture: search first.
2. News sells the course: every news item links to the matching training.
3. Dates and cities, never prices.
4. Show the ground truth: real sessions, real clients, real cities.
